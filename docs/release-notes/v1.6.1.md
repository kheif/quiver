## v1.6.1

- `quiver status` and `quiver add` no longer print every Searchable skill on one line. When a source with hundreds of skills is made Searchable at once, they name the first ten and say how many more there are; `-v` names them all.
- The release workflow retries its npm check until the installed binary runs. v1.6.0 was published correctly, but its Windows check failed once because the tarball of a platform package showed up on the registry a little after its metadata.

Nothing else changes from [v1.6.0](https://github.com/kheif/quiver/releases/tag/v1.6.0).
